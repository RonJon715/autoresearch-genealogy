# Land Patents of Lewis Burwell I (1648)

## Source

Virginia Land Patent Records, 1640s. Confirmed via Encyclopedia Virginia and secondary sources.

## Patent 1: 18 April 1648

Granted to Lewis Burwell together with Thomas Vaulx for 2,300 acres on the south side of the York River.

## Patent 2: 12 June 1648

Lewis Burwell patented 2,350 acres on the north side of the York River. This became his plantation of Fairfield (later called Carter's Creek), Gloucester County. Patent obtained by claiming headrights inherited from his stepfather.

## Patent 3: 1650

On consecutive days in 1650, Burwell received 1,600 acres in Northumberland County and two adjoining tracts of 500 acres each on the south bank of the Potomac River.

## Total Holdings

Within a decade of arrival, Burwell owned or co-owned at least 7,250 acres across multiple Virginia counties. By 1704, son Lewis Burwell II paid taxes on 26,650 acres.
