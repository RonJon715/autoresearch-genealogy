## Biography

Thomas Jordan II was born about 1634 in Nansemond County, Virginia. He married Margaret Brasseur about 1658 and became a Quaker. Margaret's family were Huguenots from France. Thomas and Margaret had numerous children. He died in 1699. His parentage, linking him to Thomas Jordan I (who arrived in Virginia about 1623), is accepted by some researchers but remains disputed. The earlier connection to Samuel Jordan has been disproven by DNA and paper trail research.
