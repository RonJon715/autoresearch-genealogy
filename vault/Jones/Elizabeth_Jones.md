## Biography

Elizabeth Jones (1724-1774) married [[Spence_Monroe_1727]] (Colonel Spence Monroe). The GEDCOM import lists many children from this union, including [[Rosamond_Rosa_Jones]], [[Spottswood_Jones]], and [[President_James_Monroe]].

### Clarification on Jones/Monroe/Spottswood Lines

Note: This file may represent a GEDCOM merger of two distinct individuals or two marriages. [[Elizabeth_Jones_Monroe]] is listed elsewhere in the vault as the same person, with spouses [[Col._Spence_Monroe_Father_of_James_Monroe_5th_U.S._Pres.]] and [[Minitree_Jones_Sr]]. The children [[Rosamond_Rosa_Jones]], [[Spottswood_Jones]], and Churchill Jones are now understood to be children of [[Minitree_Jones_Sr]] (Minitree Thomas Jones, 1723-1808) and Mary Elizabeth Spottswood (1732-1791), not of the Monroe union. The "Spottswood" given name in the family derives from Mary Elizabeth Spottswood's maiden name, with a potential connection to Governor Alexander Spotswood, Lt. Governor of Virginia 1710-1722. (Speculative; requires primary source verification.) This conflation in the GEDCOM requires further research to untangle.
