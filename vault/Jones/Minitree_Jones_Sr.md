## Biography

Minitree Thomas Jones Sr (1723-1808; GEDCOM dates: 1730-1797) married Mary Elizabeth Spottswood (1732-1791). The Spottswood family is potentially connected to Governor Alexander Spotswood, Lt. Governor of Virginia from 1710 to 1722. (Speculative; requires primary source verification.)

Their children included [[Rosamond_Rosa_Jones]] (1750-1821), who married [[William_F_Bourne_Sr]] (William Bourne, 1743-1836) in 1765 in Hanover County, Virginia; Minitree Jones Jr.; [[Spottswood_Jones]] (1754-1826); and Churchill Jones. The given name "Spottswood" used for one son derives from his wife's maiden name.

Note: The GEDCOM import lists birth as 1730 and death as 1797. The clarified connection gives dates of 1723-1808. The GEDCOM also lists [[Elizabeth_Jones_Monroe]] as his spouse; Elizabeth Jones Monroe was also wife of [[Col._Spence_Monroe_Father_of_James_Monroe_5th_U.S._Pres.]]. This overlap in the GEDCOM data requires further resolution against primary sources.
