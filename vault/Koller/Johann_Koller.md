## Biography

Johann Koller was a Donauschwabe (Danube Swabian) from the village of Liebling in the Banat region (then Austria-Hungary, now Timis County, Romania). The Koller family is documented in the Liebling Ortsfamilienbücher:

1. Johann Möhler, *Ortssippenbuch Liebling im Banat* (Altshausen, 1979), 836 pages, covering 6,303 families.
2. Adam Arnold, *Lieblinger Familienbuch* (1994; 2001 CD-ROM edition).

(Strong Signal for existence of sources; not yet directly consulted)

An Ancestry.com user tree entry (accessed via search results) identifies a later Johann Koller (b. 05 Jan 1932, Liebling) as the son of Peter Koller and Maria Hedrich, connecting the Koller and Hedrich families across generations. This Peter Koller matches [[Peter_Koller]] (b. 14 Oct 1900, Liebling) in the vault. (Speculative; user contributed tree, needs verification against Ortsfamilienbuch)

David Dreyer's Banat Ship List documents Koller family emigration from Liebling. One entry notes a 25 year old from Liebling arrived 8 Oct 1912 heading to Chicago, going to join "cousin Andreas Koller." Another entry notes a 24 year old from Liebling departed 13 Nov 1903 for St. Louis on the ship Brandenburg; notes indicate "Maria married Konrad Koller. They lived in St Louis and later in Chicago." (Moderate Signal; Dreyer Database, extracted from passenger manifests)
