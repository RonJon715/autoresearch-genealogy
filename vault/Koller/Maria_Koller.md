## Biography

Maria Koller was born on 2 April 1929 in Liebling, Timis, Romania, a Donauschwaben (Danube Swabian) village in the Banat region. Her parents were Georg Koller (b. 1902, Liebling; d. 1949) and Elisabetha Koller (b. 1906, Liebling; d. 1993).

**Immigration (Moderate Signal):** Maria emigrated from the Romanian Banat to the United States as a post-WWII displaced person, likely arriving around 1950. The Donauschwaben of the Romanian Banat were displaced beginning in 1944 during the Soviet advance. Many emigrated under the Displaced Persons Act of 1948, settling in Chicago and surrounding areas. The Lieblinger Verein Chicago, an organization of ethnic Germans from Liebling, maintained community ties in the Chicago area.

Maria married Friedrich (Fredrich) Hedrich (b. 1927, Liebling). She was preceded in death by her husband after 69 years of marriage. She died on 31 October 2018 and was buried in Chicago, Cook County, Illinois. (Dignity Memorial obituary for son Fredrich Hedrich; GEDCOM import)
