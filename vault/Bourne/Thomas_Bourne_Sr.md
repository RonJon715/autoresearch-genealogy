## Biography

Thomas Bourne was born about 1581, probably in Frittenden, County Kent, England. He married Elizabeth (surname unknown, sometimes speculatively given as Bassenden) about 1614 at Biddenden, Kent. Thomas and Elizabeth came from Kent County, England, about 1630 during the Puritan Great Migration. They settled in Marshfield, Plymouth Colony, where Thomas was a man of substance and repute. He served as Deputy from Marshfield to the Plymouth General Court in 1641, 1642, and 1645, and was "Assistant" to Governor Winslow from 1642 to 1646. He was addressed as "Mr. Bourne," indicating a person of high social rank. Thomas died on 11 May 1664 at Marshfield at the age of eighty three. His will declared his son John to be his heir and appointed him executor.
