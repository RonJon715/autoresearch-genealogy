## Biography

Friedrich Hedrich (also recorded as "Fredrich") was born on 28 April 1927 in Liebling, Timis, Romania, a village in the Banat region originally settled by ethnic German (Donauschwaben/Danube Swabian) colonists in the 18th century. His parents, Friedrich Hedrich (b. 1903) and Philippina Hedrich (nee Kind, b. 1906), were ethnic Germans who lived in the Romanian Banat.

**Immigration (Moderate Signal):** The Hedrich family immigrated to the United States as post-WWII displaced persons, likely arriving around 1950 based on the pattern established by other Liebling families. The Donauschwaben of the Romanian Banat were displaced beginning in 1944 when the Soviet Red Army approached, and many emigrated to the United States under the Displaced Persons Act of 1948. The family settled in Chicago, Cook County, Illinois, which had a large Donauschwaben community with its own aid societies and cultural organizations, including the Lieblinger Verein Chicago.

Friedrich was preceded in death by his wife of 69 years, Maria Koller Hedrich. He was the father of Maria Rink, Betty (Tom) Rogers, Fred Hedrich, Kathrine (Ron) Faulkner, Eric (Katie) Hedrich, and brother of Katarina Just. (Dignity Memorial obituary, dignitymemorial.com/obituaries/chicago-il/fredrich-hedrich-8110603)
