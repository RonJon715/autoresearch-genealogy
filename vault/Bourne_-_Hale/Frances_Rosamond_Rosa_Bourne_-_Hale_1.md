## Biography

According to B.F. Nuckolls, *Pioneer Settlers of Grayson County, Virginia* (1914), Chapter II: Frances was born June 5th, 1788, and married Stephen Hale, Sr. This confirms the vault's existing birth date (5 June 1788). (Moderate Signal; secondary source, local history)

Note: The vault lists her father and mother as Unknown, but the Nuckolls book identifies her as a daughter of William F. Bourne Sr. and Rosamond (Rosa) Jones. Her parents should be linked to [[William_F_Bourne_Sr]] and [[Rosamond_Rosa_Jones]]. (Moderate Signal; Nuckolls, Chapter II)

**DISCREPANCY**: The vault shows her marrying [[Stephen_Mason_HailHale_Sr._Colonel_War_1812_5th_great-uncle]] on 9 Jan 1834, but the Nuckolls book describes Stephen Hale Sr. without specifying the marriage date. The vault date of 1834 seems late given her 1788 birth and the number of children listed. This may warrant further investigation.
