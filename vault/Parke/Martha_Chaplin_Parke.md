## Biography

Martha Chaplin married Robert Parke on 9 February 1601/2 at Semer, Suffolk, England. She was Robert's first cousin. Martha and Robert had at least nine children baptized in various Suffolk parishes. The family immigrated to New England in 1630 with the Winthrop Fleet. Martha died in 1643 in Connecticut.
