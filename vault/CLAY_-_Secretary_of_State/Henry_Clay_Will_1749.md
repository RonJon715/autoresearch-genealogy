# Will of Henry Clay "The Elder" (1749/1760)

## Source

Will signed 28 March 1749. Probated at the September term of Chesterfield Court, 1760. Henry Clay died 3 August 1760 at The Raells, Chesterfield, Dale Parish, Province of Virginia.

## Summary of Bequests

**Son William Clay**: "My land and plantation on Deep Creek, in Henrico County, whereon Richard Belcher now lives"
**Son Henry Clay**: "The land and plantation he now lives on, and two hundred acres of land at 'Let Alone', in Goochland County, it being the Lower Survey belonging to me"

Henry Clay owned large tracts of land in Henrico, Goochland, Chesterfield, and Cumberland Counties.

## Biographical Notes

Born 3 August 1672, Dale Parish, Chesterfield (then Henrico), Virginia. Son of Charles Clay and Hannah Wilson. Married Mary Mitchell in 1709 in Henrico County. He was an Indian trader ca. 1711-1712, traveling "as far beyond Carolina as it was to it." In 1726, Henry witnessed Ralph Blankenship's will.

Henry Clay was the great grandfather of the statesman Henry Clay (1777-1852). Dale Parish was formed in 1735.

## Tombstone Inscription

"In Memory of Henry Clay Who died at dinner with his children and grandchildren at an annual festival given them on August 3, 1760." Memorial located next to Manchester High School, Bailey Bridge Road, Chesterfield County. Presented by Richmond Chapter, Colonial Dames of the 17th Century, 1957.

## Father Identified

WikiTree and Geni.com identify his father as Charles Clay (b. ca. 1640, d. ca. 1696). This updates the GEDCOM entry which listed "Father: Unknown."

## Confidence

**Moderate Signal.** Will details corroborated by WikiTree (Clay-84), Geni.com, FamilySearch, and published in *Adventurers of Purse and Person, Virginia 1607-1624/5* (3rd ed., 1987, pp. 193-197) and Filson Club Publications No. 14, *The Clay Family* (1899). Original will book not personally examined.
