# Faulkner Direct Patrilineal Line

Father to son descent from the earliest documented ancestor to the present. 16 generations, 1480 to present. 450 years from Kent, England to Chicago, Illinois.

## Migration Path

**Sussex, England (1480)** > **East Sutton, Kent, England (1575)** > **London (1628)** > **Kent County, Maryland (1665)** > **Queen Anne's County, MD (1706)** > **Granville County, NC (c.1750)** > **Iredell County, NC (c.1790)** > **Orange County, NC** > **Tishomingo/Alcorn County, MS (c.1840s)** > **McNairy County, TN (c.1900)** > **Alcorn County, MS** > **Chicago, IL (c.1970s)**

---

## Generation 1: Youngin Faulconer (1480-1515)
**Sussex, England**

Earliest documented ancestor. Born 1480 in Sussex, England. Married [[Lucy_Bewley_Falconer]] (b. 1484, Whytham, Sussex; d. 1527). One known son: [[William_Joyner_Falconer]].

Sources: Ancestry Family Trees (GEDCOM import). Confidence: Speculative.

## Generation 2: Edward Falconer (1538-1587)
**Balcombe, Sussex, England**

Born 1538 in Balcombe, Sussex, England. Married [[Anne_Bonicke]] on 5 August 1571 at Buxted, Sussex, England. Died 28 January 1587. Children: Joan Falconer, William Falconer/Faulkner, Carolus Faulkner.

Note: The connection between Youngin Faulconer and Edward Falconer has gaps in documentation. Treat these earliest two generations as speculative.

Sources: Ancestry Family Trees. Confidence: Speculative.

## Generation 3: William Faulconer (1575-1638)
**East Sutton, Kent, England**

Born 1575 in East Sutton, Kent, England. Parents: [[John_William_Faulkner]] (b. 1568) and [[Martha_Faulkner_1574]]. Married **[[Lady_Elizabeth_Filmer]]** on 6 February 1629 at St. Benet, Paul's Wharf, London.

The Filmer connection is significant: the Filmers were a baronetcy family from Kent (baronetcy created 1674). Sir Robert Filmer, the political philosopher who wrote *Patriarcha*, was from this family.

Died 18 June 1638 in St. Mary's, Whitechapel, Middlesex (or Wivelsfield, Sussex). Eight children: [[Michael_FALKINER]], [[Edmund_Faulkner_1625]], [[John_Thomas_Faulkner_Sr.]], [[Mary_Elizabeth_Faulkner]], [[Elizabeth_Faulkner_Faulconer_Falkner]], [[John_Robert_Faulkner]], [[Francis_Fauconer_2]], [[William_Falconer_2]].

Sources: England, Select Marriages 1538-1973; Manchester Church of England records; England & Wales Christening Index. Confidence: High.

## Generation 4: John Thomas Faulkner Sr. (1628-1708)
**London, England > Kent County, Maryland**

Born 1628 in London. Occupation: **draper** (cloth merchant), indicating wealth. Married **[[Elizabeth_Travis_Filmer]]** (likely a relative of his mother's Filmer family) c.1652 in Salem, Massachusetts.

**Immigrated 22 January 1665** on the ship *Agreement* to Kent County, Colony of Maryland. Brought his wife Elizabeth, sons Thomas, Francis, and John Jr., plus two servants. Paid 300 pounds of tobacco for passage, confirming merchant status.

Died 9 January 1708 in Queen Anne's County, Maryland. Nineteen children documented.

Sources: Colonial Records Search (ship *Agreement* documented across multiple sources); Ancestry Family Trees; England & Wales Christening Index. Confidence: Strong Signal.

## Generation 6: John Pleasant Falconar Jr. (1678-1727)
**Kent County, Maryland > Queen Anne's County, Maryland**

Born 1678, Kent, Maryland. Married **[[Sarah_Elizabeth_Forde_Ford_Falconer_Faulkner]]** (c.1705/1716, Queen Anne's County). Owned the plantation at **Hog Pen Neck**, initially in Kent County, redesignated Queen Anne's County in 1706.

**Will proven 9 May 1727**, Queen Anne's County (Maryland Calendar of Wills, Vol. 6). Named wife Sarah as sole Executrix; listed underage children Thomas, James, William, Emanuel, and Ann. **Estate inventory** filed as Queen Anne's County Administration Papers File No. 191.

27+ children documented. Died 9 May 1727.

Sources: Maryland Wills and Probate Records 1635-1777; Colonial Records Search Pass 2; Queen Anne's County Administration Papers. Confidence: Strong Signal.

## Generation 7: Emanuel Ford Faulkner Sr. (1718-1798)
**Queen Anne's County, Maryland > Granville County, NC > Iredell County, NC**

Born 1718 on the family plantation in Queen Anne's County, Maryland. Married **[[Elizabeth_Violet_Dickerson_1735]]** in 1750 in Granville, North Carolina.

**Lord Granville land grant, 27 November 1760:** Received 695 acres on Sandy Creek and John Dickerson's line, Granville County (later Warren County). Eventually held a 795 acre plantation with enslaved workers.

Described as **illiterate**. Migrated from Granville County to Iredell County between 1790 and 1798.

**Will of Emanuel Forkner, Iredell County (probated c.1798):** "Be it known to all men that I, Emanuel Forkner, Of Iredell County in the state of North Carolina, being in perfect mind and sound memory...I give unto my beloved wife, Elizabeth Forkner, My land and three Negroes, Eliza, Carry and Molly, and all estate of whatsoever kind I am possessed with until her death." He effectively **disinherited all but two of his ten children**. Elizabeth died later the same year.

Eighteen children documented.

Sources: FamilySearch Ancestors; WikiTree; Geni; Colonial Records Search. Confidence: Moderate Signal (will text quoted in secondary sources; no original probate book/page located).

## Generation 8: Moses Faulkner/Fortner (1754-1835)
**Granville County, NC > Orange County, NC**

Born 1754 in Bute (formerly part of Granville), North Carolina. Mother: [[Elizabeth_Violet_Dickerson_1735]]. Married **[[Susannah_Faulkner]]** (also listed as Susannah Saulter) on 19 November 1829 in Greene, Alabama. Also married [[Dorcas_Martin]].

**War of 1812 veteran.** Service records documented.

The surname shift occurs in this generation: the family used **Fortner/Forkner** interchangeably with Faulkner, reflecting phonetic spelling by census takers and court clerks.

Buried in Chapel Hill, Orange County, North Carolina. Fourteen children documented.

Sources: U.S. War of 1812 Service Records; 1800 Census (Hillsborough, Orange, NC, Series M32, Roll 34, Page 563). Confidence: High.

## Generation 9: Abel F Faulkner (c.1794-1880)
**North Carolina > Mississippi**

Born c.1794 in North Carolina. Married **[[Sarah_Franklin]]** (b. 1794). This generation marks the family's migration from North Carolina to **Mississippi**. By the 1850 census, Abel was in Tishomingo County, Mississippi (Northern Division, District 4, Roll 382, Page 4).

Twelve children documented.

Sources: 1850 and 1860 Federal Census. Confidence: High.

**Wenasoga, Alcorn County, Mississippi**
