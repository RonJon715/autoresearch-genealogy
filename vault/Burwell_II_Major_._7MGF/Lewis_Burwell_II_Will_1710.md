# Will of Lewis Burwell II (1710)

## Source

Will dated 11 October 1710, proved 10 February 1710/11 (Old Style). Recorded in York County, Virginia. Lewis Burwell resided at "King's Creek," York County, and "Carter's Creek" (Fairfield), Gloucester County.

## Summary

Lewis Burwell II died 19 December 1710 at his plantation on King's Creek in York County. The will mentions:

**Sons**: Nathaniel, James, and Lewis
**Grandson**: Lewis, son of Nathaniel
**Son in law**: Henry Seaton
**Daughters**: Joanna, Elizabeth, Lucy, Martha, Jane, and Martha junior
**Godson**: William Burwell

## Tombstone Inscription (Abingdon Church, Gloucester County)

"Here lyeth the body of the Hon. Lewis Burwell, son of Major Lewis Burwell and Lucy his wife, of the county of Gloucester, who first married Abigail Smith... by whom he had four sons and six daughters; and, after her death, to Martha, the widow of the Hon. William Cole, by whom he had two sons and three daughters, and departed this life 19th day of December Anno Domini 1710."

## Confidence

**Moderate Signal.** Will proved in York County (not Gloucester). Tombstone inscription corroborates. Multiple secondary sources agree on will contents. Primary source (original will book) not personally examined.
