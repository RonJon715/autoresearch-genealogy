# Land Patents of Major Andrew Monroe (1650-1662)

## Source

Virginia Land Patent Records. Published in *William and Mary College Quarterly Historical Magazine* Vol. 16 (1907/1908). Andrew Monroe was the father of William Monroe (b. 1666).

## Patents

**8 June 1650**: First land grant, 200 acres designated as "Head Rights" in Northumberland County. This is the earliest record of the Monroe family in Virginia.

**1650-1662**: Andrew Monroe received several additional tracts of land in Northumberland and Westmoreland Counties.

## Family

Andrew Monroe, son of David Munro of Katewell, Scotland, immigrated to Virginia in the mid 17th century. He married Elizabeth (maiden name unknown). He built Monrovia (Monroe Hall) on Monroe Creek, a tributary of the Potomac River near present day Colonial Beach, Westmoreland County.

Andrew Monroe died in 1668, leaving issue: Susannah, Elizabeth, Andrew (2), George, and William. William Monroe (b. 1666) was the ancestor in the family tree who left a will in 1737.

## Connection to Family Tree

Andrew Monroe (d. 1668) was the father of [[William_Line_to_pres_Monroe_6th_Gt_Monroe]] (b. 1666, d. 1737). William's grandson Spence Monroe (1727-1774) was the father of President James Monroe (1758-1831).

## Confidence

**Moderate Signal.** Land patents are official Virginia records. The Monroe genealogy is well documented in published sources, including the *William and Mary Quarterly*. However, this note is compiled from secondary sources; the original patent books at the Library of Virginia have not been personally examined.
