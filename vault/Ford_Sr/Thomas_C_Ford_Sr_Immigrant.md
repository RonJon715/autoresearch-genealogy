## Biography

Thomas Ford entered Maryland circa 1650. A servant "Thos Ford" was transported to Maryland "before June 1652," listed among those William Ayres claimed for transporting to the colony on 5 October 1653. The earliest confirmed record placing Thomas in Anne Arundel County is the survey of the tract called "Fordstone" on 17 November 1659, located near Herring Creek. He married Eliza (surname unknown). His estate was inventoried circa October 1682 in Anne Arundel County, which is likely when he died. His son James Ford (1666-16 February 1703) of Herring Creek had a will dated 28 July 1702 (proved 16 December 1702) leaving to wife Eliza the "Upper Plantation" during life, and to sons John and James 300 acres called "Rockford" on the south side of the Susquehanna. (Sources: Geni.com; WeRelate Person:Thomas_Ford_(63); Skordas, *Early Settlers of Maryland*)
