## Biography

According to B.F. Nuckolls, *Pioneer Settlers of Grayson County, Virginia* (Bristol, TN: King Printing Co., 1914), Chapter II: Stephen Bourne was born in 1744 (also given as 1743 in some records) to Stephen and Hannah Bourne of Louisa County, Virginia. He was a brother of William F. Bourne Sr. and John Bourne; all three brothers were Grayson County pioneers. They grew up on the 300 acre land grant obtained by their grandfather, William Bourne II, in 1719. (Moderate Signal; secondary source, local history)

The vault shows his death as 8 Jun 1836 at Knob Fork, Grayson, Virginia. His brother William F. Bourne Sr. also has death recorded as 8 Jun 1836. This likely represents an error in the GEDCOM; the same death date for two brothers is suspicious and needs verification. (Note: possible data entry error or merge confusion)
