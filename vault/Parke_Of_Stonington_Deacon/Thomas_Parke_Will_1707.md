# Will of Thomas Parke Sr. of Preston, Connecticut (1707)

## Source

Will dated 5 September 1707. Witnesses took oath 9 August 1709. Recorded in New London Probate Records, vol. A-B, 1675-1716, image 226. Also found in Hartford Probate Packets, Palmer-Peck, 1675-1850, images 606-13.

## Summary of Bequests

**Wife Dorothy**: Received the estate. Ten sheep specifically bequeathed to her.
**Son John**: Named executor. Had already received real estate by deed dated 24 August 1693. Real estate to return to John upon Dorothy's death.
**Samuel (son of deceased son Thomas)**: Ten shillings
**James (son of deceased son Robert)**: Ten shillings
**Son Nathaniel**: Eight pounds
**Son William**: Eight pounds; also named executor with Ebenezer Witter
**Daughters Martha, Dorothy, and Alice**: To receive the remainder

## Inventory

The inventory of Thomas Parke, who died 30 July 1709, was taken on 8 August 1709 and sworn to by his widow Dorothy on 9 August 1709.

## Biographical Notes

Thomas Parke, born 13 February 1615/16 in Hitcham, Suffolk, England. Son of Robert Parke and Martha Chaplin. Immigrated 1630 with the Winthrop Fleet. Married step sister Dorothy Thompson on 20 October 1644 in Wethersfield, Connecticut.

He owned land in Stonington on the east bank of the Mystic River, purchased from his brother in law Rev. Richard Blinman. In 1680, he disposed of this land and moved to Preston. In 1698, he, with sons Robert and John, organized the first Church of Christ in Preston and became its first deacon.
