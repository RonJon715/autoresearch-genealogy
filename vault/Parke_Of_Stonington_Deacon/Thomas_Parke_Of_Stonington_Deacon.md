## Biography

Thomas Parke, born 13 February 1615/16 in Hitcham, Suffolk, England. Son of Robert Parke and Martha Chaplin (married 9 Feb 1601/2 at Semer, Suffolk). Immigrated 1630 with the Winthrop Fleet. Married his step sister Dorothy Thompson on 20 October 1644 in Wethersfield, Connecticut. He owned land in Stonington on the east bank of the Mystic River, purchased from his brother in law Rev. Richard Blinman. In 1680, he sold this land and moved to Preston. In 1698, he, with sons Robert and John and nine others, organized the first Church of Christ in Preston and became its first deacon, Rev. Mr. Treat being its pastor. His will (5 September 1707, proved 9 August 1709) names wife Dorothy, sons John, Nathaniel, and William, deceased sons Thomas and Robert (via their sons Samuel and James), and daughters Martha, Dorothy, and Alice. He died 30 July 1709 in Preston, New London County, Connecticut. (Sources: WikiTree Parke-8; Connecticut Probate Records, New London vol. A-B, image 226; TAG vol. 82 no. 4)
