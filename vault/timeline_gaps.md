# Timeline Gap Analysis

Systematic comparison of expected life events against existing vault records for 25 priority ancestors. Each person who lived during a period when records were created should have left a documentary footprint. This analysis identifies where those records are missing from the vault.

## Summary

| Metric | Count |
|---|---|
| Persons analyzed | 25 |
| Total expected events | 251 |
| OPEN (missing records) | 216 |
| FOUND (documented) | 29 |
| NOT_APPLICABLE | 6 |
| High priority OPEN gaps | 136 |

## Methodology

Expected events were generated based on:
- Birth and death dates from Family_Tree.md (all GEDCOM imported, unverified)
- U.S. Federal Census conducted every 10 years (1790 onward; 1890 census destroyed by fire)
- Vital records (birth, marriage, death certificates) availability by state and era
- Military service eligibility based on age and conflict dates (Civil War 1861 to 1865, WWI 1917 to 1918, WWII 1940 to 1945)
- Property and probate records for persons who lived past age 21

Priority levels:
- **High**: Census records from 1850+ (named individuals), vital records in well indexed jurisdictions, military draft registrations
- **Medium**: Pre-1850 census (statistical only), burial records, pre-vital-records era church records
- **Low**: Property and tax records
