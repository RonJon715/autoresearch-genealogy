## Biography

John Meador (Meadows) Sr. (ca. 31 July 1658, ca. 21 November 1721) was born in Charles Parish, York County, Virginia. Son of Thomas Meador Jr. ("orphan") and Sarah (surname unknown) of Hoskins Creek. He held 50 acres on the south side of the Rappahannock River in Old Rappahannock County (later Essex County). He married first Elizabeth White (daughter of Richard White and Addra, ca. 1677/78), who died 17 August 1694. He married second Mary Awbrey (daughter of Richard Awbrey and Dorothy North) after 1694. He had thirteen children by two wives. His will (proved ca. 1721, Essex County) divided his 450 acre property (originally granted to his father as an orphan) among the children of both wives. Daughter Dinah received only one shilling, "apparently because of her husband." His son John Meador Jr. (d. 14 May 1720) left a nuncupative (verbal) will naming William Bourne and Thomas Meador as executors, demonstrating the Bourne/Meador family connection. (Sources: WikiTree Meador-3; Bodie, *Historical Southern Families* Vol. IV, pp. 139-145; Meador & Meador, *Our Meador Families in Colonial America*, 1983)
