## Biography

Peter Garland II, known as "the Mariner," was born about 1595 in Petworth, Sussex, England. He married Elizabeth Coles (daughter of George Coles and Alice Sergeant) on 3 October 1625 at Petworth, Sussex. He was first found as a resident of Charlestown, Massachusetts Bay, in 1637. He is believed to be the ancestor of all the early settlers named Garland in both the Northern and Southern States. However, there is significant genealogical confusion between multiple Peter Garlands of this era, and the identification of him as the father of the Peter Garland who settled in Virginia remains disputed. WikiTree notes that "Ancestry genealogies have confused Peter the son of Peter and Elizabeth Coles with Peter the son of Ambrose."
