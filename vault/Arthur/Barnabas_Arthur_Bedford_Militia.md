# Barnabas Arthur Jr.: Bedford County Militia Records

## Source

Bedford County militia records. Also referenced in Karen J. Dunn, *Bedford County, Virginia Militia 1774-1783: Identifying Bedford County Militia Companies During the American Revolutionary War* (2009).

## Military Service

"Barnabus Arthur Jr." appears on Bedford County militia rolls as a soldier who was engaged in warfare with the Indians, French, and British before the Declaration of Independence, and continued service during the Revolutionary War.

## DAR/SAR Patriot Status

No specific DAR Patriot Index number was found for Barnabas Arthur in this search. However, the Bedford County militia records are recognized by the DAR and SAR for patriot service verification. Further research in the DAR Patriot Ancestor Database at dar.org is recommended.

## Designation "Junior"

The designation "Jr." in colonial records indicates relative age in the community, not necessarily a father/son relationship. However, given that Barnabas Arthur's father was John Arthur Sr. (not Barnabas), the "Jr." likely distinguishes him from an older Barnabas in the same community.

## Related Arthur Family Military Service

The Arthur family had extensive military involvement in Bedford County. Sir Thomas Barnabas Arthur (b. 1658, County Antrim, Ireland; d. 1725, Bedford County) was the patriarch who settled in Virginia. Multiple Arthur men served in frontier defense and the Revolutionary War.
