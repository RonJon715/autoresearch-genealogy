# Unresolved Persons

Named individuals extracted from vault person files, biographies, wills, land records, and the Research Log who do not currently have their own person files. Cross referenced against all 2,274 person files and Family_Tree.md on 2026-03-20.

## Methodology

1. Read all 2,274 person files and Family_Tree.md.
2. Identified all named individuals mentioned in biographies, children lists, document references, and discrepancy notes that are NOT wikilinked to existing person files.
3. Categorized each individual using the scheme below.
4. Searched web sources (WikiTree, Geni.com, Find a Grave, FamilySearch, published genealogies) for "Likely family" individuals.
5. Logged all searches (positive and negative) in Research_Log.md.

## Categories

- **Known**: Has a person file or appears in Family_Tree.md with a wikilink.
- **Likely family**: Appears in multiple documents, shares a family surname, or holds a documented family role (parent, child, sibling, spouse). Warrants further research.
- **Community connection**: Appears once in a single document (neighbor, witness, official). No evidence of family tie.
- **Cannot identify**: Name too common or insufficient context to distinguish from other individuals of the same name.

## Recommendations

1. **Highest priority for person file creation**: The ten Jordan sons (Thomas III through Joshua) are well documented in Boddie and Hinshaw, appear as siblings of the existing [[Elizabeth_Jordan]] in the tree, and have Strong Signal confidence. Creating person files would extend the Jordan branch significantly.

2. **Second priority**: The Bourne daughters (Martha, Margaret, Elizabeth, Ann, Lydia) and Elizabeth Bassenden are documented in Thomas Bourne's will and corroborated by WikiTree and stanleyhistory.net. The Family_Tree.md currently says "and daughters" without listing them individually.

3. **Third priority**: Robert Brasseur (father of Margaret Brasseur Jordan) is well documented as a Huguenot settler and would connect the Jordan line to French origins.

4. **Fourth priority**: The Parke siblings (Robert Jr, William, Anne, Samuel) are documented in Robert Parke's will and WikiTree. William Parke and Samuel Parke have Strong Signal confidence.

5. **Research needed**: Elizabeth Buckner's maiden name remains the most promising open question. The Long family connection in St. Mary's Parish / Caroline Co. should be investigated through county deed books and order books.

6. **No action recommended**: The Garland parentage dispute (Ambrose Garland vs. Peter Garland II) cannot be resolved with currently available online sources. The community connections (Governor Winslow, John Bradford, Josiah Winslow, Robert Waterman, Nehemiah Smith, Nathaniel Tilden) are not direct ancestors and do not warrant person files unless the tree scope expands.
