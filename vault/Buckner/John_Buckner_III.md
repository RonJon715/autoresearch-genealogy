## Biography

John Buckner III, son of John Buckner and Ann Ballard, was born about 1695 at Old Rappahannock, Virginia. He received a deed of gift dated 14 July 1727 from Ann Buckner for the lands in his possession in Essex County formerly belonging to his late father. He married Elizabeth (surname unknown); her neighbors, the Longs, frequently witnessed legal documents for her, making them leading candidates for her birth family. John died before 13 June 1740 when his will was probated in the Caroline County VA court by his executrix and widow Elizabeth. Elizabeth later divided the land between sons William and Thomas in 1750 and transferred additional land to son George in 1752, suggesting the children were still minors at the time of John's death.
